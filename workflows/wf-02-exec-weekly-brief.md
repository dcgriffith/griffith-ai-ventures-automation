# Workflow 02 — Weekly executive brief

**ID:** `wf-exec-weekly-brief`  
**Trust:** Green  
**Agent:** Executive Strategy  
**Orchestration:** Grok weekly schedule (Sunday)

## Steps

1. Summarize known activity: content published, product progress, sales data if provided.
2. Top 3 priorities for next week.
3. Risks (scope, no SKU, brand drift).
4. Decisions needed from owner.
5. Assign tasks to Marketing / Revenue / Product agents (as prompts, not auto-executing).

## Success metric

Brief produced weekly; owner completes at least 2 of 3 priorities or documents why not.
